# Demo script (about 90 seconds)

1. **Open with one real story** (sourced, not invented): older adults lose large sums to business and government imposters; phone calls are a common first contact (FTC 2024 spotlight).
2. **Home** — point to calm layout, large buttons, "I need help now".
3. **Practise** — start Fake bank fraud call, Easy to spot, Be the target. Show the practice banner. Send one reply, then **Hang up and see how I did**.
4. **Debrief** — highlight numbered moments and praise for stopping.
5. **Check a message** — paste the demo bank text (or use a photo). Show risk label, marked phrases, what to do / what not to do.
6. **My family** — if signed in, show Waiting vs Accepted; otherwise show share-link fallback from Help now.
7. **Scams near me** — show weekly summary and the "not verified" label.
8. Close: remind judges `DEMO_MODE=true` works without a Groq key.
