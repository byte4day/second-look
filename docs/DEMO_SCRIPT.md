# Demo script (about 90 seconds)

Keep to three beats: **Check → Practise → Help now**. Family and Scams near me are optional if time remains.

1. **Open with one real story** (sourced): older adults lose large sums to business and government imposters; phone calls are a common first contact (FTC 2024 spotlight). Say: “Scammers are professionals. Stopping is always a good move.”
2. **Home** — point to the **Scam Shield** brand, large buttons, and always-visible **I need help now**.
3. **Check a message** (money shot, ~30s)
   - Tap **Use a scam text** (or upload the Downloads photo — words auto-fill).
   - Show risk label, marked phrases, what to do / what not to do.
   - Optional: tap **Use a normal text** to prove DEMO is input-aware (not one canned scam).
4. **Practise** (~30s) — Tap **Start the bank fraud practice now**. Show the yellow practice banner. One reply, then **Hang up and see how I did**. On debrief, praise for stopping is first.
5. **Help now** (~15s) — numbered calm steps + share by text.
6. **Optional** — Scams near me already has seeded weekly bars (labelled “not verified”). Family has SMS/WhatsApp share without signing in.
7. **Close** — `DEMO_MODE=true` runs without any key; real AI runs through Unorouter.
